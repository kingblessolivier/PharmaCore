{% extends "documents/base.html" %} {% load documents %} {% comment %} An invoice, in the arrangement supplied — and a tax invoice, which is a statutory document on top of being a commercial one. The layout gives: company block and INVOICE heading, invoice number / date / terms / due date, BILL TO beside SHIP TO, numbered lines with item and description, notes and terms bottom-left, and the money bottom-right down to BALANCE DUE. The statutory part is what the previous version was missing, and it is not optional: * **the customer's TIN** — a VAT-registered buyer cannot reclaim input VAT without it, so an invoice that omits it is worthless to them; * **the taxable amount and the VAT stated separately**, and broken down by rate — a line at 18% and an exempt line cannot be summed, because the buyer reclaims against the standard-rated portion only; * **the unit** each quantity is counted in. Amounts arrive pre-formatted. Arithmetic in a template is a second, invisible implementation of the ledger, and the two drift. {% endcomment %} {% comment %} The heading reads INVOICE, as the supplied layout has it.
