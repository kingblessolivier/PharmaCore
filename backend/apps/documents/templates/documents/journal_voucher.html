{% extends "documents/base.html" %} {% comment %} A journal voucher, laid out the way an accountant expects to receive one. What it is *for* drives the shape. A journal voucher is the authorisation slip for a posting that no invoice, receipt or delivery produced — most often one a human typed. It is the piece of paper an auditor asks for when they want to know why a number moved, so it has to answer four questions on its face: what was posted the account split, code and name, debit against credit why the narration, and the document that caused it by whom named, with room for the second signature that authorises it is it still true posted, or reversed by a later entry — and which one The previous version answered the first and roughly half of the second. It had no totals check, no amount in words, no reversal state, no signature block worth the name, and it printed `str(Decimal)` — "1500.00" where a voucher says "1,500.00". It was a table with a heading, not a voucher.
