What it is *for* drives the shape: it is the moment custody passes, so it has to record who handed over, who accepted, and whether what arrived matched what was sent. The previous version had the lines and none of that — no supplier, no order reference, no unit, no value, no remarks, and nowhere for the two people present to sign. A goods receipt nobody signed is a list, not evidence. Two things are kept that a general-purpose GRN would not have, because this is medicine: **batch and expiry on every line**.
