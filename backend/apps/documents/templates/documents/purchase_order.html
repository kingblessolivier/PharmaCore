A purchase order is an offer to buy, and a supplier has to be able to accept it and ship against it without asking a question — so it states who to invoice, where to deliver, how the goods travel, on whose terms, and what unit every quantity is counted in. "10" means ten cartons or ten tablets, and a supplier guessing wrong sends 24,000 tablets or ten. Two constraints from the renderer shape the markup: * xhtml2pdf supports neither flexbox nor grid, so "comments on the left, totals on the right" is a table, not a `display: grid`. * It cannot nest a table inside a table cell — it computes a negative available width and produces no page at all. That bottom block is therefore ONE table: the comments cell spans the totals rows beside it.
